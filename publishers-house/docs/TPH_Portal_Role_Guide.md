# The Publishers House — Portal Role Guide
*Understanding what every person can see and do on the portal*

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This document is designed to be shared with church administrators, pastoral team members, department leaders, and unit heads so everyone understands their privileges, responsibilities, and workflows on the digital portal (**thepublishershouse.org/portal**).

---

## Overview: How the Portal Works

The portal serves as the centralized digital operational command for The Publishers House across both **Jos Headquarters** and **Abuja Campus**. Access is role-governed and hierarchical — privileges determine exactly which dashboards, datasets, and action centers a user sees.

There are **seven distinct access tiers**:

| Role Tier | Primary Audience | Key Access Level |
|---|---|---|
| **🟣 Super Administrator** | Rev. Joshua Agunbiade | Unrestricted oversight across all 14 admin modules, financial ledgers, and system governance |
| **🔵 Administrator** | Pastoral Staff & Senior Leadership (e.g. Pastor Boniface Onah) | Full administrative operations, member directory, attendance, pastoral pipeline, and communications |
| **🟡 Dual-Role Pastors** | Pastors heading specific units (Pastor Ngbede, Pastor Theophilus, Pastor Damilare) | Tri-level 1-click header switcher: `[ Admin Dashboard ]` `[ Unit Command ]` `[ Member View ]` |
| **🟢 Multi-Unit Leaders** | Leaders heading multiple units/clusters (Ifedayo Mary Agunbiade, David Jatto) | Dedicated multi-unit pill switcher (`[ Welfare ] [ Children ]` or `[ Protocol ] [ Transport ] [ Sanctuary ]`) |
| **🟢 Unit Heads** | Single unit appointed leaders (e.g. Ushering, Worship, Secretariat, Security) | Unit Command Center: Rosters, volunteer turnout, swap approvals, unit announcements, and assets |
| **🟠 Unit Workers / Servants** | Active volunteers rostered in one of the 13 units | Member portal + My Unit tab: View duty rosters, file swap requests, see fellow servants |
| **⚪ General Members** | Every registered believer | Personal portal: Service check-in, online giving, prayer petitions, testimonies, courses, and settings |

## 1. 🟣 Super Administrator — Rev. Joshua Agunbiade

**Primary Account:** Rev. Joshua Agunbiade (`joshuaagunbiade@gmail.com`)

The Super Administrator possesses master privileges across every layer of the system. This role provides apostolic and executive oversight of all church operations.

### What Super Admin Can Do:
* **Full Member & Identity Management** — Add, edit, verify, impersonate (preview mode), deactivate, or permanently delete any member or administrator.
* **Role & Privilege Assignment** — Promote/demote administrators, appoint unit heads, assign multi-unit leadership clusters, and manage security access.
* **Master Financial Oversight** — Complete ledger visibility into tithes, offerings, seeds, building projects, and Paystack transactions across Jos & Abuja campuses.
* **Monthly Executive Ministry Report Generator** — Generate, review, print, or export comprehensive monthly church reports (`/portal/reports`) summarizing attendance, finance, 13-unit rosters, and pastoral care.
* **Church Units Architecture** — Create, configure, or adjust any of the 13 ministry units; reassign leadership and modify default meeting schedules.
* **Attendance Master** — Log, modify, and review Sunday and Midweek service attendance, headcount breakdowns (men, women, children, online streamers), and comparative analytics.
* **Pastoral Care & Secretarial Inquiries** — Unrestricted access to first-timer follow-ups, at-risk member lists (absent 3+ consecutive weeks), and pastoral inquiry responses.
* **Communications & Broadcasts** — Send instant push notifications, lock-screen alerts, and email broadcasts church-wide or targeted to specific segments.
* **System Audit Trail** — Review timestamped logs of every administrative action, role modification, or data change.
* **Growth Track & Curriculum** — Oversee Discipleship Academy courses, lesson materials, and member progress.
* **Facility & Asset Governance** — Manage sanctuary spaces, equipment maintenance, and facility reservations.

> [!NOTE]
> The Super Administrator account is cryptographically protected and cannot be deleted, demoted, or locked out by any other administrator.

## 2. 🔵 Administrator — Pastoral Staff & Senior Team

**Who:** Pastor Boniface Onah and designated senior administrative staff.

Administrators have full operational access to manage day-to-day church activities, coordinate assemblies, and support the pastorate.

### What Administrators Can Do:
* **Member Directory** — Register new members, update contact details, assign service units, and export directory data to CSV.
* **Attendance Operations** — Log official service headcounts, review comparative attendance charts, and monitor volunteer turnout.
* **Pastoral Care Pipeline** — Track first-time visitors from initial visit to discipleship integration; manage counseling inquiries.
* **Monthly Ministry Reports** — Generate and print monthly operational reports (`/portal/reports`) for secretarial meetings and pastoral council reviews.
* **Church Gatherings & Events** — Create, schedule, and publish services, conferences (Festival of Light, Merismos), and special meetings.
* **Communications** — Broadcast push alerts and email announcements to the congregation or specific units.
* **Testimony & Praise Moderation** — Review submitted testimonies, verify accuracy, and approve them for public bulletin display.
* **Finances & Giving** — View transaction logs, verify Paystack references, and monitor category totals.
* **Facility Reservations** — Approve sanctuary booking requests and track church technical assets.

### What Administrators Cannot Do:
* Delete or demote the Super Administrator.
* Modify system-locked audit trails or fundamental core configuration.

MULTI-UNIT LEADERSHIP & OVERSIGHT:
[ Welfare ✓ ]   [ Children ]      — (For Ifedayo Mary Agunbiade)

[ Protocol ✓ ]   [ Transportation ]   [ Sanctuary ] — (For David Jatto)

* **Dynamic Context Re-indexing:** Selecting a unit pill immediately updates the entire Command Center, Roster, Volunteer List, Attendance Roll-Call, Assets, and Announcements to reflect that specific unit.

* **Dual Header Switcher:** Easily toggle between `[ Unit Command ]` and `[ Member View ]` with persistent unit state.

## 5. 🟢 Unit Heads — Single Unit Appointed Leaders

**Who:** Appointed leaders heading individual ministry units across the 13 departments:

| Unit Name | Department Scope | Special Privileges |
|---|---|---|
| **Secretariat Unit** | Documentation, church records, registration | **Special Authorization:** Full access to `/portal/reports` to generate the Monthly Ministry Report |
| **Media & Technical** | Audio engineering, livestream, lighting, projection | Asset custody logging, technical equipment checks |
| **Worship Team** | Vocalists, choir, instrumentalists | Rehearsal call sheets, setlist coordination |
| **Ushering Unit** | Sanctuary seating, offering collection, guest greeting | Service station assignment, collection audit coordination |
| **Protocol Unit** | Pastoral itinerary, guest ministers, VIP coordination | Executive lounge prep, guest logistics |
| **Welfare Unit** | Compassion ministry, benevolence, member visitation | Visitation logs, benevolence distribution follow-ups |
| **Sanctuary Unit** | Facility cleanliness, altar arrangement, hall preparation | Pre-service sanitation checks, inventory management |
| **Editorial Unit** | Publications, doctrinal transcription, bulletin | Article editing, sermon transcription review |
| **Follow-Up & Hospitality** | First-timer welcoming, phone follow-up, reception | Handover to Discipleship upon completed follow-up |
| **Prayer Unit** | Pre-service intercession, prayer chains, vigils | Prayer request intake, intercessory watch schedules |
| **Children's Ministry** | Kids church, teachers, child safety | Parent check-in coordination, age-grade curricula |
| **Transportation Unit** | Campus shuttles, logistics, fleet movement | Route management, vehicle maintenance |
| **Discipleship & Evangelism**| Believers' Foundation School, street & campus outreach | Growth track admissions, outreach assignments |

### What Unit Heads Can Do:
* **Duty Roster Management (`/portal/unit/roster`)** — Assign volunteer members to specific duty dates and stations; set duty roles (e.g. Lead Sound Engineer, Station A Usher).
* **WhatsApp Group Broadcast Helper** — 1-click action to generate clean, pre-formatted duty rosters ready to copy and paste into the unit's WhatsApp group.
* **Individual Volunteer WhatsApp Reminder** — 1-click pre-filled personal WhatsApp reminder sent directly to a scheduled worker.
* **Unit Attendance Roll-Call (`/portal/unit/attendance`)** — Mark scheduled workers as Present or Absent; see automated flags for workers who attended general church service but were off-duty.
* **Duty Swap Approval (`/portal/unit/swaps`)** — Review, approve, or deny duty swap requests submitted by volunteers.
* **Unit Volunteer Roster Expansion** — Search the directory and add registered church members directly to their unit.
* **Unit Push Announcements** — Send priority push notifications directly to the devices of all members in their unit.
* **Unit Asset Inventory (`/portal/unit/assets`)** — Log microphones, communion supplies, badges, and departmental tools.

### What Unit Heads Cannot Do:
* View or alter data of any unit outside their assigned jurisdiction.
* Access financial giving ledgers (except Secretariat head for executive reports).
* Delete church members from the general directory.

## 6. 🟠 Unit Workers / Active Servants

**Who:** Any registered member actively assigned to serve in one of the 13 church units.

Unit workers enjoy all capabilities of general members, plus an exclusive **My Service Unit (`/portal/member/unit`)** operational workspace.

### What Unit Workers Can Do:
* **View Personal Duty Schedule** — Check upcoming service assignments, call times, and specific duty roles.
* **Request Duty Swaps** — If unable to serve on a scheduled Sunday, file a formal swap request naming an available replacement volunteer.
* **Unit Fellowship Directory** — View fellow volunteers serving in the unit and contact leadership for briefing questions.
* **Receive Priority Unit Alerts** — Receive lock-screen push notifications about rehearsal call times, duty briefings, and urgent notices.
* **Access Unit SOP Manuals** — Download unit operational guidelines and decorum checklists.

### What Unit Workers Cannot Do:
* Approve swap requests or alter the master roster.
* Add or remove members from the unit.
* Send official unit announcements.

## 7. ⚪ General Members — Every Registered Believer

**Who:** Every registered member and worshipper of The Publishers House.

General members access the **Member Portal (`/portal/member`)**, designed to nourish their spiritual walk, facilitate fellowship, and simplify church participation.

### What General Members Can Do:
* **Member Dashboard** — View personalized service countdowns, upcoming conference announcements, and recent doctrinal essays.
* **Resume Audio Teachings** — Continuous audio player bar remembering listening progress across sermon expositions.
* **Personal Attendance Logging** — Record personal attendance during Sunday celebrations and Thursday doctrine services.
* **Giving History & Online Tithing (`/portal/member/giving`)** — Give tithes, offerings, and project seeds securely via Paystack; view personal contribution history.
* **Submit Prayer Petitions (`/portal/member/prayers`)** — Send prayer requests marked as "Pastoral Confidential" or "Church Altar Intercession".
* **Share Testimonies** — Submit praise reports detailing divine interventions for pastoral review.
* **Growth Track & Discipleship (`/portal/member/courses`)** — Enroll in modular faith-building courses and track completion progress.
* **Pastoral Inquiries & Counseling (`/portal/member/inquire`)** — Send inquiries directly to the pastorate or submit applications to join any of the 13 ministry units.
* **Settings & Push Notification Controls (`/portal/settings`)** — Update personal profile details, upload member photos, change passwords, and configure granular notification preferences.

## 8. 🔔 Cross-Cutting System Capabilities

### A. High-Priority Lock-Screen Push Notifications
* **Persistent Delivery:** Delivers alerts to Android phones, iPhones, tablets, and laptops even when the app or browser is completely closed.
* **Heads-Up & Lock-Screen Integration:** Notifications pop up directly on the device lock-screen with distinct vibration patterns and direct links to the relevant portal page.
* **Granular Preference Toggles:** Members can customize their alerts under `/portal/settings`:
  * ⛪ Church Services & Special Gatherings
  * 🛡️ Duty Roster & Unit Call Times
  * 📖 Pastoral Word & Fresh Audio Messages
  * 🔥 Altar Prayers & Approved Testimonies
  * 💬 Pastoral Inquiries & Care Responses

### B. Real-Time Cross-Device Synchronization
* **Zero-Cache Inconsistency:** Profile changes, unit reassignments, or role updates made by administration update across open devices (phones, tablets, laptops) live via background polling and window-focus listeners, eliminating stale local data.

### C. Monthly Executive & Secretariat Ministry Report Generator
* **Centralized Sector Aggregation (`/portal/reports`):** Accessible by Super Admin, Administrators, and the **Head of Secretariat Unit**.
* **Comprehensive Monthly Coverage:**
  1. Executive KPI Ribbon (Total Members, Net Monthly Giving in ₦, Service Turnout).
  2. Complete 13-Unit Roster & Leadership Status.
  3. Date-by-date Sunday & Midweek Headcount Ledger (Men, Women, Children, Online).
  4. Financial Giving Ledger (Tithes, Offerings, Seeds, Building Project, Paystack reconciliations).
  5. Pastoral Care Funnel (First-timers followed up, at-risk members contacted, counseling inquiries).
  6. Discipleship Enrollment & Academy Progress.
  7. Governance & Administrative Audit Trail.
* **Executive Print & Clipboard Ready:** 1-click clipboard summary copy for quick executive distribution and `@media print` styling for clean, physical A4 PDF printing.
